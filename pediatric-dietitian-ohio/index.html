Ohio Licensed 100% Virtual RD-Led Care

If you're searching for a pediatric dietitian in Ohio, you likely want convenient, credentialed care without a long drive to a hospital or clinic. Healthy Little Me is a fully telehealth practice founded by Nisrine Hamad, a Registered Dietitian Nutritionist who completed advanced clinical training at Nationwide Children's Hospital in Columbus and holds a Master's degree in Pediatric Nutrition and Dietetics from The Ohio State University.

Every visit happens over a secure video call, Monday through Friday, 10:00 AM–5:00 PM, so Ohio families in Columbus, Cincinnati, Cleveland, Dayton, Toledo, Akron, and everywhere in between can get the same specialized pediatric nutrition support without needing to live near a children's hospital.
