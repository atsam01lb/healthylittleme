Eating Disorder Nutrition Therapy

Pediatric & Teen Eating Disorder Dietitian, 100% Telehealth

Specialized, HAES-informed nutrition therapy for children and teens with anorexia, bulimia, binge eating disorder, ARFID, OSFED, and other disordered eating patterns — delivered entirely by secure video visit.

Book an Appointment Meet Nisrine
HAES-Informed Family-Based Telehealth Only

Nutrition therapy is one part of a larger eating disorder treatment team, alongside therapists, physicians, and sometimes psychiatrists. As the dietitian on that team, Nisrine focuses specifically on rebuilding a healthy, sustainable relationship with food: structured meal planning, monitoring nutritional adequacy, and coaching families through mealtime challenges at home.

Every plan is grounded in a Health At Every Size (HAES) and weight-inclusive philosophy — the goal is consistent, adequate nourishment and reduced food-related anxiety, not a number on a scale. Nisrine coordinates directly with your child's other providers so nutrition care stays connected to the rest of their treatment.

Binge Eating Disorder & OSFED

Structured, non-restrictive nutrition support to reduce binge episodes and normalize eating patterns.

Orthorexia & Disordered Eating

Support for rigid or fear-based food rules that interfere with everyday life, school, or family meals.
